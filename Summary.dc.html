Recommendations: Clear Value, Simple Path

Three swings at clarity. Do these before shipping any new feature.

🔴 P0 — Do First (Copy + Layout)

1. Home page: Replace "Where stories become opportunities" with the loop

Show three simple steps: (1) "Brands post real briefs" ($5K pool), (2) "Creators submit work" (126 entered), (3) "Blind votes pick the best" (winner gets $2K). Lead with social proof: "$84K paid to creators this month." ONE primary button: "Create or vote now" (then split by role on login).

2. Login: Show what each role CAN DO

Change radio cards from "Creator / Brand / Both" to show ACTIONS. Creator: "📹 Enter challenges, win money." Brand: "📤 Post briefs, see blind votes." Both: "Do all of it." Add small icon + one-line benefit to each.

3. Challenges list: One strong CTA per card, add sticky "How to"

Replace passive click-to-open with explicit "Enter this challenge" button on every card (visible on hover, always on mobile). Add sticky bar at top: "First time? (1) Pick a challenge, (2) Upload your video, (3) Wait for voting results." Status badges ("funded", "round1_open", "resolved") → human text ("Accepting entries until 3pm", "Round 1 voting now", "Winner announced").

4. Challenge detail: Show prize breakdown inline (not in sheet)

Prize section: "Winner: $2,000 | Runner-up: $800 | 3rd: $300 | Crowd favourite: $200" (single row, no "see more"). Show current round status: "🎬 Round 1: Accept teasers (2 days left)." Creators see "Submit a teaser". Brands see "View analytics / Invite creators".

5. Results page: Outcome as headline, not detail

Top of page, large and clear: "🎉 You won $2,000!" or "Advanced to Round 2" or "Didn't advance (16th place, 342 votes)". THEN show breakdown. Action button below: "Share your win" or "Enter another challenge."

🟠 P1 — Do Second (CTAs + Visibility)

6. Challenge card CTA scope: Creator vs. Brand vs. Voter

Same card, three contexts. Creator sees "Enter now". Brand sees "View analytics" (clickable only if they own the challenge). Voter sees "Vote for Round 2" (if round is open). No guessing.

7. Voting round: Show impact statement

VoteDeck cards: Add under the pair "Your vote decides who advances to Round 2 (top 10 of 126)." After voting: "You've voted 12/30 times today. Voting streak: 4 days! 🔥"

8. Brands: Surface "Create a challenge" from everywhere

On /challenges list, add sticky pill-button: "Create new challenge" (for sellers/both only). On challenge detail owned by brand, show "Invite creators" link + "View analytics" link side-by-side.

9. Empty states: Tie them to actions

"No payouts yet" → Button: "Browse challenges to start earning" (/challenges). "No challenges" → Button: "Create your first brief" (/challenges/new for brands). No passive text without next step.

🟢 P2 — Polish (Micro-copy)

10. Status badges: Use plain language

"funded" → "Prize secured" / "round1_open" → "Accepting entries" / "resolved" → "Winner announced". Every badge should answer "Can I enter?"

11. Form errors: Replace Mux debug strings

Any upload error: "Upload didn't go through — try again in a moment." Add "Need help?" link to FAQ.

12. Rally link: Explain the benefit

Button text: "Share & earn rally bonus" (not "Copy link"). Tooltip: "Votes from this link count extra if your round is still voting."

The North Star: One-Sentence Clarity

Every page should answer these three questions clearly: (1) What am I looking at? (the current round/status) (2) What can I do? (submit, vote, create, share) (3) What happens next? (timeline, payouts, next round). If a visitor can't answer all three in 10 seconds, we're hiding the value.
