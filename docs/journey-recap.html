MOMENT — the journey, as built

A creator-competition marketplace: brands fund cash-prize challenges, creators compete through blind peer voting and public voting, and the crowd decides who gets paid. Below is every persona's real path through the app today — not a plan, the actual wired screens.

Creator

competes to get paid

The core loop the whole product is built around — show up, get judged blind, get paid real money.

1

Lands, signs up / → /login

Sees the pitch, picks "Creator," verifies by phone OTP — no password, ever.

2

Finds a challenge /discovery · /map

Browses live campaigns and top creators, or explores the 3D-style presence map of who's active right now.

3

Enters round 1 /challenges/[id]/submit

Submits a teaser. A checklist auto-screens obvious violations before it ever reaches a human.

4

Judges peers blind /rounds/[id]

Votes on paired teaser entries with no names shown. An attention-check pair guards vote quality; a participation gate keeps the round from stalling.

5

Advances, submits full content, votes again

Round 2 repeats the blind-vote pattern on full submissions. Composite score = peer votes + optional seller score, weighted.

6

Round 3 goes public /rounds/[id]

Finalists are named. Anyone — logged in or not — can vote. The brand may set a final pick, or the top quality-vote finalist wins by default.

7

Gets a share card /results/[submissionId]

A dynamic win/advance/knockout card with a live vote count and a rally link — recruiting spectators to vote counts toward crowd favourite, never the prize itself.

8

Gets paid /wallet

Winner pool, stipend, survivor bonus, or crowd favourite — transferred via Stripe Connect the moment it's earned, shown as earned before Stripe even settles it.

9

Builds a track record /me · /notifications

Tier badge, voting streak, rally stats, a referral link that pays $5 the first time someone they invite acts — and a real inbox for round results and payouts.

Brand

funds the challenge

Trust has to be earned fast here — this is where real money enters escrow.

1

Signs up as a brand /login

Same phone-OTP flow, role picked up front — not buried after the code's already sent.

2

Drafts a challenge /challenges/new

Title, brief, checklist criteria, prize pool — saved as a draft before any money moves.

3

Verifies, then funds /challenges/[id]

A KYB gate blocks funding (not drafting) until reviewed. Funding shows one total by default — full pool/stipend/survivor-bonus/fee itemization sits one tap behind "see breakdown."

4

Round 1 opens itself

The funding wizard polls for the Stripe webhook and opens round 1 automatically — no separate "go live" step to remember.

5

Invites specific creators /challenges/[id]/invite

Pulls from the discovery feed, sorted by tier, and drops a real notification into their inbox.

6

Watches the funnel /challenges/[id]/analytics

Submissions → advances → votes-per-round → payouts-by-type, in one view. No view tracking yet — that's flagged, not faked.

7

Rates, builds reputation

Once resolved, rates the finalists; public trust stats (on-time payout rate, campaigns run) accrue for the next creator deciding whether to enter.

Spectator

no account needed

The growth surface — every one of these pages works with zero login, on purpose.

1

Arrives via a shared link /v/[code] · /r/[code]

A creator's rally link (vote for me) or referral link (join MOMENT) — same personal code, two different jobs.

2

Watches the battle /battle/[challengeId]

Server-rendered, cached, rate-limited independently of the rest of the API so a viral spike can't degrade voting or payments.

3

Votes, gets asked to verify

Casting a public vote requires login, but the vote's destination is remembered (?returnTo=) so verifying returns straight to the finish, not back to square one.

4

Explores freely /discovery · /map

Same public feed and live map a logged-in user sees — nothing about browsing is gated.

What ties the three journeys together

Two-currency voting

Quality votes decide the prize; rally votes (from a recruited spectator) only ever decide crowd favourite. One can't buy the other.

Tiers & streaks

Bronze → Platinum from lifetime earnings and wins. Voting streaks pause — never break — when the platform itself has nothing to vote on.

Installable PWA

Manifest, app-shell service worker, a custom install prompt that waits for a real second visit instead of ambushing the first.

Presence & identity

A generated placeholder avatar with a tier-colored presence ring, honestly labeled as a placeholder — not oversold as AI.

Where this actually stands

Core loop— create → fund → submit → vote → vote → payout — fully wired, real Stripe escrow.
Growth loops— rally links, referrals, share cards, discovery, live map — all live.
Retention— tiers, streaks, notifications inbox, wallet — all live.
Admin console— the one piece left: god-view, force-reveal, KYB queue, dispute resolution.
