MOMENT App — UX Flow & Value Prop Analysis

Three user personas, one core loop. Identifying clarity gaps, engagement points, and CTA opportunities.

The Core Value Prop

For creators:

"Fair briefs. Blind votes. Real money."

Compete in live challenges where your work is judged by quality, not followers. Win cash if you place in the top positions.

For brands:

"Original work. Real voting. No ads."

Post a brief, fund the prize, watch creators compete. You get authentic work and direct voting data—no followers, no algorithms.

For voters:

"Vote blind. Pick the best. Shape outcomes."

Your vote decides winners in blind peer voting. No creator names, no follower counts—just pure work quality.

CLARITY GAPS — What's Confusing?

Landing page says "Where stories become opportunities"

Clear? No. What ARE the opportunities? How do I get paid? When? How much?

Login asks "I'm joining as..." but doesn't explain what's different

Creator vs. Brand vs. Both — what can each person DO? Why does it matter?

Challenges list shows status badges ("funded", "round1_open", "resolved")

What do these MEAN to a creator? Can I still enter? Is voting happening?

Prize pool shown but breakdown hidden behind "see breakdown"

How much do winners actually get? What's the cut between rounds? Why hide it?

Teaser vs. Full submission — what's the difference? Why submit twice?

Is there a penalty for going straight to full? Are both required?

Voting shows two videos side-by-side with "pick faster"

Is this the only round? What happens after? Who sees my vote?

KEY ENGAGEMENT POINTS — Where Do We Lose People?

Moment 1: First visit (no login)

Landing page headline + 2 CTAs. Loss: Users don't understand the mechanic. Fix: Show the loop in 3 steps, with numbers ($84K paid this month). Make "Start your story" the ONE primary CTA.

Moment 2: Role selection (login step 1)

Three radio-card options. Loss: Users pick "Both" by default because it "sounds better". Fix: Show what each role CAN DO (Creators: enter challenges, win money. Brands: post briefs, see voting results). Add a small icon showing the main action.

Moment 3: First dashboard (challenges list)

Challenge cards with status + prize + entry count. Loss: Users don't know what to click or how to "do" anything. Fix: Lead with the strongest challenge card (one with active voting, close deadline). Add a sticky "How to enter" banner at top.

Moment 4: Challenge detail (decision to enter)

Prize breakdown + "Submit an entry" CTA. Loss: KYB gate blocks brand accounts. Fix: Pre-check KYB before redirecting to login. Show "Entry phase" clearly (teaser or full?) so creators know what to submit.

Moment 5: Submission upload (creator enters)

Drag-drop zone. Loss: Upload fails silently or with Mux debug string. Fix: Clear success state ("You're entered!" with next step). Show "Round 1 closes in 3 days" to set expectations.

Moment 6: Voting (the core loop)

VoteDeck cards. Loss: No submission media wired in (shows blank/placeholder). Fix: Get real media into the API response FIRST. Then show vote impact: "Your vote counts toward the top 10 advancing to Round 2."

Moment 7: Results (creator finds out they won)

Results card with live vote count. Loss: Unclear if you won or advanced. Fix: Headline should be ONE of: "You won! $2,000 coming soon" or "You advanced to Round 2" or "Eliminated (16th place, 342 votes)". Make next step clear.

CTA AUDIT — Are They Clear?

Page Current CTA Problem Recommendation
/ (home) "Start your story" + "Watch live" Two equal buttons. Unclear which is for whom. One big "Create or vote now" + optional secondary
/login "Start your story" (same text as home) Generic. Doesn't change based on role selected. "Enter this challenge" or "Create a challenge" based on role
/challenges Click a card, or "New challenge" (hidden for creators) Non-obvious. Creators see no actions they can take. Floating "Enter a challenge" on any card. Brands see prominent "Create new"
/challenges/[id] "Submit an entry" (large button) Good, but hidden if KYB gate blocks. Brands see nothing they can do. Show CTA for BOTH roles: Creators "Enter now", Brands "View results/analytics"
/rounds/[id] Vote button (implied in cards) No text. Unclear what happens after you vote. No next-step CTA. "Vote" + after: "View results" or "Enter this challenge"
/results/[id] Share result + "Vote for me" link Secondary. Main outcome not emphasized. Headline says outcome (Won/Advanced/Eliminated) THEN "Share this" or "Enter next challenge"
/wallet Empty state "No payouts yet" Discouraging. No action offered. "Browse challenges" link in empty state CTA
/discovery Tab toggle + avatar rows with no action per row Browsing is passive. No "invite" or "view profile" offered. Per-row action: "View profile" or (for brands only) "Invite to challenge"
