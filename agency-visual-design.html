Design System Principles

Dark Aesthetic

Deep charcoal (#0a0e14) ground with off-white typography. Sophisticated, premium. Subtle gradients and soft glows.

Electric Accent

Cyan (#00d9ff) stops the scroll. Used for calls-to-action, active states, and key data highlights. Feels AI/futuristic.

Semantic Colors

Green (#4ade80) for growth metrics, Red (#ef4444) for cost/decline. Non-accent semantic colors maintain hierarchy.

Generous Spacing

Large whitespace between sections. Breathing room. Refined spacing system creates premium feel. No cramped layouts.

Thin Borders

1px subtle borders on cards. Elevated surfaces with slight transparency. Glass effect used selectively, not everywhere.

Editorial Typography

Oversized headlines with tight line height. Geometric sans for display. Clean sans for body. Monospace for data/metrics.

This design system balances creative power with performance clarity. Every section is visually distinctive while maintaining one coherent visual language. The first impression communicates: Creative Power + AI + Performance + Trust.
